# Art

The four images here are placeholders: a coloured circle with the companion's initials and one simple shape each. They stand in until the author's original character art arrives.

- `tt.svg`: orange, with a spark, for the bold move.
- `ks.svg`: green, with three small blocks, for the economics under the story.
- `mm.svg`: blue, with a rising arc, for thinking bigger.
- `mr.svg`: plum, with two linked rings, for the people and the evidence.

The demo page (`docs/demo.html`) draws the same placeholders inline, so it needs no image files.

When the real art arrives, it replaces these files under the same names. All art in this repo must be original: nothing is copied or traced from another product's characters, and no image is made to look like the real people the companions are inspired by.
