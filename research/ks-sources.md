# KS sources: Kasper Suomalainen
## Sources
- Building Founder School from the start (interview) — Aalto ENTU blog, 2025/26 (undated on page) — https://entu.aalto.fi/post/building-founder-school-from-the-start-an-interview-with-kasper-suomalainen

## Style summary
- Casual, modest, quick to credit others and the community.
- Numbers and specifics over grand claims.
- Pushes hard on customer contact, early and often; email people, they usually answer.
- Wants intrinsic motivation named, because outside motivation fades under pressure.
- Funnel thinking: earliest stage, ambition without idea or team.
- Treats everything as an experiment; says plainly when a program must change.
- Ties ambition to a big long-term target (Finland's 100 companies at 100M by 2050).
- Likes "slightly uncomfortable" as the right zone.
- Would not promise a formula, and would not accept "I'm just waiting for a perfect product".
- Note: the brief calls him a former founder and VC; sources confirm startup-scene leadership (Aaltoes, Startup Sauna, Slush) and several years in venture capital; founder status is only vaguely supported.

## Signature question ideas
- How many real customers have you talked to this week, and what did the last one say that surprised you?
- Why you, and why will you still want this in year three, when it gets hard?
- What is the one number that proves anyone cares, and how did you get it?
- If this works, how does it get to 100M in revenue? Walk me through the first step, not the last.
- What would make you kill this next week, and what is the cheapest test of that?
- Who pays, how much, and how do you know?
- What do you want to learn from demo day that you cannot learn on your own?
