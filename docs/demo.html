Sprint companions

Four AI companions in Claude Code that help Founder Sprint sprinters turn their sprint into a demo-day story, in their own words. If the sprinter opts in, they keep up quietly while she builds, and spar with her only when she asks.

This page is illustrative. Leena and her clinic-scheduling startup, Slotbird, are fictional. The conversations are written to the product's real rules: one question per turn, her words quoted exactly, nothing decided for her. They are not a recording of a live session. Lines marked Behind the scenes are notes for you, the reader, not what the companions show.

Meet the companions

Each is inspired by a member of the Aalto Founder Sprint team, and says so the first time you meet.

TT

inspired by Tai Tran

Ideas, brainstorming, “what if”, and your very first session.

Energetic and opinionated. Goes for the crazy version, cuts it to one thing you can test this week, and says when a call might be wrong.

Leave with an idea you'll try this week.

I'm TT, an AI companion inspired by Tai Tran. I'm not him, and I don't speak for him.

KS

inspired by Kasper Suomalainen

Product, business model, pricing, unit economics, money.

Calm and grounded. Asks who pays, how much and how often, then where the number on your slide comes from.

Know who pays you, and why.

I'm KS, an AI companion inspired by Kasper Suomalainen. I'm not him, and I don't speak for him.

MM

inspired by Mårten Mickos

Fundraising, investors, the pitch, ambition, the honest check.

Approachable at first, more direct as he gets to know you. Turns “how much should we raise?” into “why raise at all?”, then asks for the proof.

Walk into demo day knowing why you're raising.

I'm MM, an AI companion inspired by Mårten Mickos. I'm not him, and I don't speak for him.

MR

inspired by Maija Renko

Team, cofounders, hiring, people, what the research says.

Warm and research-minded. Asks what customers did rather than said, says how sure she is, and never makes up a citation.

Know what your team and your evidence are telling you.

I'm MR, an AI companion inspired by Maija Renko. I'm not her, and I don't speak for her.
